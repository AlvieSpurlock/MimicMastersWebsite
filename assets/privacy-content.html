MIMIC MASTERS

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: October 10, 2026

1. SUMMARY

Mimic Masters is a free, ad-supported memory game for iOS and Android. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no game server run by us. Almost everything the game records stays on your device.

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Your saved progress, settings and birth month and year are kept only on your device.
  • Location is optional, is offered only to players aged 13 or older, is used only on your device and only on the day it is collected, and is deleted the next time you open the game after that day.
  • Advertising partners receive device and advertising data so they can show ads. This is the main way information leaves your device.
  • You can limit ad tracking at any time using the controls described in Section 5.
  • The game sends error reports, app logs and performance numbers to Sentry, a diagnostics service, so we can fix bugs and crashes. These contain no name, contact details, location or birth date (Section 3.10).

This policy covers the Mimic Masters mobile app. By playing, you acknowledge the practices described here.

2. WHO WE ARE

3. INFORMATION THE GAME HANDLES

This section lists every kind of information the game touches, where it is kept, and whether it leaves your device.

3.1 Birth month and year

  • When: you enter it once at first launch. We do not ask for the day.
  • Where it is kept: on your device.
  • Leaves your device: No. Only a yes/no signal that a player is under the age threshold is passed to the advertising software.

3.2 Game data

Scores, high scores, play counts per mode, daily streak, achievements, lives, in-game currency earned through play, and playtime totals.

  • When: as you play.
  • Where it is kept: on your device.
  • Leaves your device: Your saved game data does not. Summary numbers for each run (mode, score, rounds reached, run length, lives lost and continues used) are included in diagnostics data, see Section 3.10.

3.3 Settings and onboarding answers

Volume, vibration, language, light or dark mode, accessibility options, notification choices and play-style answers.

  • When: when you choose them.
  • Where it is kept: on your device.
  • Leaves your device: Your saved settings do not. Which settings and accessibility options are turned on, setting changes, and the play-style answer you chose are included in diagnostics data, see Section 3.10.

3.4 Location

GPS coordinates and the nearest city and state.

  • When: only if you are 13 or older and grant the location permission.
  • Where it is kept: on your device. It is used only on the day it is collected and is deleted the next time you open the game after that day.
  • Leaves your device: Not sent by us. See the note on advertising partners in Section 5.

3.5 Notification schedule and permission status

  • When: if you allow notifications.
  • Where it is kept: on your device.
  • Leaves your device: No. Reminders are scheduled locally. We do not use a push server or collect a push token.

3.6 Advertising and device data

IP address, advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), device model, operating system, language, time zone, app version, ad views and taps, your consent choices, and general location inferred from IP address.

  • When: when ads are requested or shown.
  • Where it is kept: collected directly by advertising software in the app.
  • Leaves your device: Yes, to Appodeal and its advertising network partners.

3.7 Estimated ad revenue totals

  • When: when an ad is shown.
  • Where it is kept: on your device.
  • Leaves your device: No. Used only to display the in-game charity contribution estimate.

3.8 Count of times the donation link was opened

  • When: when you tap it.
  • Where it is kept: on your device.
  • Leaves your device: No. The link itself may carry a tag showing the visit came from Mimic Masters. It does not identify you.

3.9 Standard web request data

IP address, device type and operating system type.

  • When: when the game downloads translation text, recommendation lists or images.
  • Where it is kept: with the web host that serves the file, including Google for files hosted on Google Sheets.
  • Leaves your device: Yes, as with any internet request. We do not receive or log it.

3.10 Diagnostics and performance data
Error and crash reports from the game's code (error message, the code location where it happened and the in-game steps leading up to it), app warning and error logs, performance measurements (frame rate, frame time, memory use, garbage collection), run summaries per game mode, how you move through the game (screens opened, buttons tapped, modes chosen, tutorial steps completed or skipped, reward offers accepted or declined, settings and accessibility options in use and changed, play-style answer, permission prompts accepted or declined, but not the permission's data), counts of ads loaded, shown, failed and completed, device model, operating system and version, screen size, language, app version, the game mode and screen in use, and a random installation ID created by the diagnostics software.

  • When: while the game is running, and when an error happens.
  • Where it is kept: sent to Sentry, a service operated by Functional Software, Inc. (sentry.io), which stores it for us. Reports that could not be sent yet are held briefly on the device and sent on the next launch.
  • Leaves your device: Yes, to Sentry. It does not include your name, contact details, advertising identifier, location, city, birth date or age. The installation ID is random, is not linked to your identity or your advertising identifier, and is reset if you delete the app. Your IP address is used to deliver the data but is not stored with it. Log lines that could mention your city or age group are removed on the device before anything is sent.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise birth date, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, health data or payment information. The game has no chat, no user profiles and no user-generated content.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply.

5. ADVERTISING, TRACKING AND YOUR CHOICES

Mimic Masters shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through Appodeal, an ad mediation service operated by Appodeal, Inc. Appodeal passes ad requests to third-party advertising networks, which may also receive the advertising and device data listed in Section 3. See the Appodeal Privacy Policy at https\://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/ for the data its software collects and the partners it works with.
Location and advertising partners. We do not send your location to anyone. However, Appodeal states that its software may collect GPS location when an app has been granted the location permission. If you grant location access to Mimic Masters, advertising software in the app may be able to read it. You can deny or revoke the permission in your device settings, and the game remains fully playable.
Personalized and non-personalized ads. With your permission, ads may be tailored using your advertising identifier and activity across other apps and websites. Without it, you still see ads, but they are not based on that tracking.

Your controls:

  • Consent form. Where privacy law requires it, a consent form appears before ads load. Where it applies, a Privacy Options button in the main menu lets you change your choice at any time.
  • iOS tracking prompt. On iOS, the game asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking. Change it under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID. Reset or delete it under Settings > Privacy > Ads (the path varies by device).
  • Location. Revoke it under your device's app permission settings.
  • Notifications. Turn each reminder type on or off in the game's Settings, or disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose whether to watch one in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Third-party links. The game can open external pages, including app store listings, map links for local businesses, forms and a charity donation page. Those sites are run by others and follow their own privacy policies.

6. CHILDREN

Mimic Masters is a general-audience game. At first launch, a neutral age screen asks for birth month and year. The answer is stored only on the device and is never sent to us.

For a player who is under 13, or whose age is not known:

  • Ad requests are flagged as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent, so advertising partners are instructed not to serve personalized ads.
  • The location feature and the local recommendations that depend on it are never offered.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from the player. The game has no accounts, chat or profiles through which a child could share it.
  • Diagnostics data (Section 3.10) is still sent, because it is needed to keep the game working. It contains no name, contact details, location or birth date, and its random installation ID is used only to count how many devices an error affects, never for advertising or to build a profile.

Some countries set a higher age of digital consent, up to 16. Where a consent form is required, players below the local age should have a parent or guardian make the choice.

Parents and guardians can remove all information the game has stored by deleting the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at madteapartydevelopment@outlook.com and we will help.

7. STORAGE, RETENTION, SECURITY AND TRANSFERS

Where data lives. Game data, settings, birth month and year, and location are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We operate no server that holds player data.

How long it is kept:

  • Location: used only on the day it is collected. It is deleted automatically the next time you open the game after that day, or when you delete the app.
  • Everything else stored by the game: kept until you delete the app or clear its data. On Android you can also use Settings > Apps > Mimic Masters > Storage > Clear storage.
  • Diagnostics data: kept by Sentry for the retention period of our Sentry plan, then deleted automatically. This is at most 90 days.
  • Data collected by advertising partners: kept according to their own retention policies.

Backups. Your device's operating system may include app data in backups you have enabled, such as iCloud or Google backups. Those backups are controlled by you and your platform provider.
Security. On-device data is protected by your device's own security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers. Sentry processes diagnostics data in the United States. Advertising partners may process data in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws may differ from those where you live. Where required, they rely on legal transfer mechanisms such as standard contractual clauses.

Deleting the app, or clearing its storage, permanently erases everything the game has saved.   • Categories collected through advertising software: identifiers, internet or network activity, and general location.
  • Categories collected through diagnostics software: a random installation ID, device information and app usage and performance data. It is used only to operate and fix the game, and is never sold or shared for advertising.

  • To opt out, decline or revoke tracking using the controls in Section 5.
  • We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. The game plays the same either way.

You may use an authorized agent to make a request. We may need to confirm the request relates to you before acting on it.
