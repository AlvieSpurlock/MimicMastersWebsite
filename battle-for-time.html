Battle For Time

Race the clock. Every sequence buys you time.

Free to play on iPhone and Android, coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How It Works

You start with 3:00 on the clock. Clear sequences to keep it alive: every 5 sequences adds 30 seconds.

When the clock hits 0:00 the run is over, no matter how many lives you have left. Run out of lives first and it's over too.

The clock keeps running while the sequence plays and after a mistake, so speed matters as much as memory.

Controls & Scoring

  • Tap the pads in the order they lit up.
  • The clock pauses for the 3-2-1 countdown, the pause menu and ad breaks.
  • Reviving after time runs out puts 30 seconds back on the clock.
  • You score 1 point for every sequence you complete.
  • You start with a set number of lives. A wrong tap costs one, and the same sequence replays so you can try again.
  • Clear sequences in a row to build a streak. The board reacts more the longer it runs.
  • Keep clearing sequences to earn free lives along the way.
  • Before a run you can pick up a Shield (blocks one mistake) or Double Life by watching an optional ad.
  • Out of lives? You can revive and carry on from where you were, also with an optional ad.

Tips & Strategy

  • Accuracy saves time. A mistake replays the whole sequence while the clock keeps ticking.
  • Count down to your next bonus. Every 5th sequence is worth 30 seconds.
  • Use the pause button if you need a moment. The clock waits for you.

Accessibility

  • The clock pauses whenever you open the pause menu.
  • Colour-blind filters for 8 types of colour vision, including two at once.
  • Light Sensitivity Safe Mode turns off flashy effects and dims the lights.
  • Motion Sickness Mode hides the moving video background.
  • Dark Mode, volume, mute and vibration are all in Settings.

See every option on the Accessibility page.
