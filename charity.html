Charity Dedication

Mimic Masters supports the Autistic Self Advocacy Network (ASAN).

About ASAN

ASAN is a nonprofit run by and for autistic people. It works to make sure autistic people have a say in the policies and decisions that affect their lives, and promotes inclusion, accessibility and respect.

Learn more at autisticadvocacy.org.

Why We Donate

Mimic Masters is designed for every kind of mind, from its colour-blind filters and light-sensitive options to its gentle, clear rules. Supporting a charity led by autistic people is a natural extension of that. If you're enjoying the game, part of the time you spend playing gives back.

How Much & How Often

A share of the game's ad revenue is set aside for ASAN. The in-game charity counter shows a running estimate of what has been raised so far.

The game itself never asks you for money and never handles donations.

How You Can Help

Want to give directly? Donate on ASAN's own site: autisticadvocacy.org/donate. There's also a Donate button in the game that takes you to the same page.

Just playing helps too. Every ad you see adds to the total.
