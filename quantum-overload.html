Quantum Overload

Eight colours. One life. No second chances.

Free to play on iPhone and Android, coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How It Works

The hardest mode in the game. Eight colours are on the board, and each one has to be tapped a set number of times, in order, Cadence Tracker style.

You get one life. One mistake ends the run.

Double Life gives you three lives here, not two. Life upgrades don't apply, so everyone starts on equal footing.

Controls & Scoring

  • Tap each colour as many times as it flashed, in order.
  • You score 1 point for every sequence you complete.
  • A Shield still blocks one mistake.
  • Out of lives? You can revive with an optional ad.

Screenshots & Video

Screenshots and gameplay video are coming soon.

Tips & Strategy

  • Master Cadence Tracker first. The skills carry straight over.
  • Grab a Shield before the run. It's your only safety net.
  • Chunk the sequence into small groups of colours and counts.

Accessibility

  • Colour-blind filters for 8 types of colour vision, including two at once.
  • Light Sensitivity Safe Mode turns off flashy effects and dims the lights.
  • Motion Sickness Mode hides the moving video background.
  • Dark Mode, volume, mute and vibration are all in Settings.

See every option on the Accessibility page.
