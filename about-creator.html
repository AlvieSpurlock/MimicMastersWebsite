About The Creator

Hi, I'm Alvie Spurlock, the solo developer behind Mimic Masters, publishing as March Hare Games from Lubbock, Texas.

My Story

I'm a gameplay and tools programmer, and Mimic Masters is a love letter to the classic colour-memory game. I wanted to take that simple idea and see how many fresh twists it could hold: counting, racing the clock, shuffling the board, beating the element instead of copying it.

Every mode, setting and system in the game was built by one person.

Why I Make Games

I make games I'd want to play, and games anyone can play. That comes down to a few rules I hold every project to:

  • No pay-to-win, no season passes and nothing designed to drain your wallet.
  • Accessible by default: colour-blind friendly, playable without sound cues, gentle on light-sensitive players, with big, easy-to-hit buttons.
  • Clear rules anyone can pick up, explained by playing rather than reading.
  • Calm, balanced feedback that never overwhelms.
