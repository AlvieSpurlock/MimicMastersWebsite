Parry Madness

Same pattern, no hesitation. Every tap is on the clock.

Free to play on iPhone and Android, coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How It Works

It plays like Classic, with one big difference: a timer runs on every single input.

Wait too long between taps and it counts as a miss. Remember the pattern and react fast.

Controls & Scoring

  • Tap each pad in order before the timer runs out.
  • You score 1 point for every sequence you complete.
  • You start with a set number of lives. A wrong tap costs one, and the same sequence replays so you can try again.
  • Clear sequences in a row to build a streak. The board reacts more the longer it runs.
  • Keep clearing sequences to earn free lives along the way.
  • Before a run you can pick up a Shield (blocks one mistake) or Double Life by watching an optional ad.
  • Out of lives? You can revive and carry on from where you were, also with an optional ad.

Tips & Strategy

  • Have the next colour ready in your head before you tap the current one.
  • Keep your finger close to the middle of the board so every pad is a short reach.
  • If the clock feels too tight, Practice Aids can add extra time per tap for a run.

Accessibility

  • Practice Aids can give you more time per tap for a run.
  • Colour-blind filters for 8 types of colour vision, including two at once.
  • Light Sensitivity Safe Mode turns off flashy effects and dims the lights.
  • Motion Sickness Mode hides the moving video background.
  • Dark Mode, volume, mute and vibration are all in Settings.
