Transparency

Every Cent, Out in the Open

I give 70% of Mimic Masters net revenue to the Autistic Self Advocacy Network (ASAN) at the end of every month. This page shows, month by month, what the game made after the stores took their cut, what I gave, and what I kept.

Google Play & App StoreFree to play, supported by ads you can tune or turn off.
itch.ioA pay-once version for $2.99, with no ads at all.
Platform cutsEach store keeps a share before anything reaches me. It's shown so you can see where every dollar goes.

These are actual payouts, recorded once the money lands. They're separate from the in-game charity counter, which is a live estimate.
