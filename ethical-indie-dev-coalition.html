Ethical Indie Dev Coalition

A coalition of indie developers who promote one another's games and hold themselves to a higher standard. Browse the games below, or enter your city and state to see games popular in your region first. Indie dev yourself, or know one who belongs here? Use the buttons beside the search.

Our Criteria

Every studio in the coalition meets all four:

  • High-quality games.
  • At least 10% to a grassroots charity.
  • No controversies in the last year.
  • All For One, One For All.
    1. Be for the player, by the player.
    2. Promote each other's games equally.
    3. Don't let petty disagreements escalate. Resolve them with dignity, clarity, and etiquette.
